# TA 3.1c -> TA:Recoil: what was converted, and every place it deliberately differs

Branch `ta-values` (from `ta-stat-review` = main d06fed81 + tools). Source values: Cavedog archives (totala1.hpi,
ccdata.ccx, btdata.ccx, Cavedog_units.ufo, rev31.gp3), layered in that order; see HANDOFF.md for how they were read.
Every changed line in `units/*.lua`, `Weapons/*.lua`, `gamedata/movedefs.lua` and the unit scripts carries a trailing
`-- TA 3.1c (was <old value>)` comment, so `git grep "TA 3.1c"` lists them and the old BOTA value is one look away.

## What now matches TA 3.1c

- **Units (259 of 266 changed, 1126 values)**: metal/energy cost, build time, build power (workertime), build range,
  hit points, armored damage modifier, speed, acceleration, brake rate, turn rate, sight/radar/sonar ranges and jammers,
  energy/metal production, energy upkeep, metal extraction, storage, cloak costs and decloak distance, cruise altitude,
  transport capacity/size, kamikaze distance. Builders and commanders produce energy again (Commander 25 E/s).
- **Weapons (492 values over 156 weapons)**: range, reload, damage, area of effect, edge effectiveness, projectile
  speed/start speed/acceleration, missile turn rate and flight time, energy/metal per shot, accuracy, spray, tolerance,
  burst and burst rate, coverage, paralyze time.
- **Per-target damage**: TA listed damage per target unit; reproduced exactly with 32 armor classes
  (`gamedata/ArmorDefs.lua`, generated by `tools/ta-data/armor.py`): e.g. AA does its TA bonus to aircraft again,
  the Immolator does 10 by default but 200/150/80 to Flash/Warrior/Peewee, the sniper 5000 to a Sumo, mines barely
  hurt their own kind (no chain reactions), the Stunner stuns only Core units and the Neutron only hurts Arm units.
- **Weapons BOTA had merged are TA's separate weapons again** (`split_weapons.py`): each commander has its own laser
  (Arm 60 / Core 70 damage), the Viper and the HLT have different heavy lasers, Jeffy/Skeeter/Skimmer (and Weasel/
  Searcher/Scrubber) each have their own light laser, both missiles of the Hawk and Vamp, the two mobile anti-nukes.
  Death explosions are TA's again (Bulldog/Goliath/Reaper/Sumo: big_unit(ex); energy storages, metal makers, Thunder,
  Geothermal...). 10 BOTA defs nothing uses any more were removed.
- **Cannon ballistics**: each cannon's own TA velocity under TA's gravity (112 elmos/s^2, the value 85 of the 95 TA
  maps use; fixed per weapon so arcs don't change with the map). BOTA instead used gravity 0.25 and recomputed every
  velocity from the range, so def velocities were dead.
- **Area of effect** is TA's: BOTA's post-processing that widened every explosive weapon with an edgeeffectiveness
  (AoE / (1 - edge), up to 160) is gone.
- **Turret aim speeds**: all 56 that differed now match TA (Lua scripts, `math.rad(<TA deg/s>)`), e.g. towers that the
  Lua ports had given one generic 286 deg/s, Penetrator 8/4 deg/s, Doomsday main gun 10 deg/s.
- **Movement water depth**: kbots and tanks wade 12 deep again, Spider 16, Krogoth 12; small ships need 3, big 15.
- **Wreck and heap health** (`Features/**`, 491 values; user report: wrecks turned into heaps far too easily): BOTA
  had most at a quarter of TA's (factories and some buildings raised instead, sunken heaps 36000). Now TA's own
  feature values (391, `-- TA 3.1c (was ...)`); where TA has none (aircraft and ship heaps, a few units without a
  TA corpse) TA's pattern (`-- TA rule`): wreck = 0.8 x the unit's TA HP (TA: 0.80-0.84 for 222 units), heap = its
  wreck. Typical wreck now ~4x tougher than before. Wreck metal is TA's too (80 changed): TA's own value, else TA's
  rule (wreck = 0.8 x the unit's metal cost, heap = half its wreck: exact for 231 / 170 TA units; 49 changed).
  Where TA points a unit at another unit's wreck (Goliath -> the Diplomat's, Informer -> the Arm jammer's) the
  unit's own unused TA feature is used (corgol_dead: 2276 HP, 558 metal; was 482 from the Diplomat's).
- **No idle regeneration** (TA had none; the engine default is 10 HP/s): `idleautoheal = 0` on every unit.

## Deviations (deliberate, with the reason)

| Area | TA | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beam lasers (Annihilator, Penetrator, Doomsday, Krogoth) | instant beams | `LaserCannon` bolts | User decision: TA had no continuous BeamLaser; beam-only tags removed |
| E.M.G. (Peewee, Warrior, Brawler, Krogoth arm cannon) | straight, no gravity | `Cannon` with TA gravity | User decision: Recoil's EmgCannon is poorly maintained. At EMG speeds the arc is nearly flat (v^2/g ~ 4-8x range) |
| Cannon projectile size | TA sprites | 2.5 + 13.5 x sqrt((dmg - 8) / 2392): 2.5 (EMG) .. ~5.5 (130 dmg tank) .. 16 (Intimidator) | User request; checked in game (GPU screenshots) |
| Laser look | TA palette lines | one look scaled by damage: thickness 1.5-4, white-hot core in the laser's tint, flare 5-10 | User request; also fixes BOTA colours with components of 4 |
| Laser bolt length (`duration`) | TA 0.01-0.03 on some | BOTA's (10 weapons) | visual only; left with the laser look |
| Bomb weapons `commandfire = 1` | set | not set | In Recoil it would make bombers manual-fire only (D-Gun style) |
| Waterline (30 units) | TA | BOTA | depends on how the remodelled S3O sits in water |
| Footprints (18 units) | TA | BOTA | sized to the remodels and the build grid |
| Movement max slope (91 units) | TA numbers | BOTA's (within ~20 %, same proportions) | TA's slope numbers are not degrees; Recoil's are |
| Water depth: commanders, amphibious tanks, crawling bombs | 100 | 400/250/200 | Recoil maps' seas are much deeper than TA's; at 100 they could not cross water they are meant to |
| Water depth: medium ships, heavy tanks, KBOTGOOD class | mixed per unit | BOTA class value | units sharing one movedef disagree in TA; would need new classes |
| `upright` on 5 subs | 1 | unset | no effect for submarines in Recoil |
| Aircraft armor | TA lists | one class per side | TA's AA lists predate Hunter, Typhoon, Seahawk, Albatross (they would take no AA bonus) |
| Construction decay | none | on (engine default) | User decision |
| Sight | TA radius, plus an altitude bonus | TA radius, terrain occlusion, no altitude bonus; air sight 1.5x (engine default) | the TA altitude formula is not documented; Recoil has no equivalent. See BALANCE_NOTES |
| Repair / resurrect cost | ? | free / 0.5 x energy (engine defaults) | not checked against TA |
| `firestarter` (Viper laser 80 vs 90) | 80 | 90 | cosmetic (chance to set trees on fire) |
| Peewee script | — | aims from the elbow until the arms are up | its idle forearms put the muzzles under ground; a Cannon will not fire from there (the old EmgCannon did not check) |
| Plane acceleration (fighters, bombers, scouts) | `Acceleration` | `maxAcc` = 3 x TA acceleration | Recoil's planes ignore `acceleration` (armed ones used a flat 0.065) and approach top speed against drag; 3 x gives TA's time to 90 % of top speed (`FLIGHT=1` probe) |
| Acceleration / brake rate below 0.01 (18 ground and sea units: Turtle, Bear, Warlord, Millennium, Crock...) | TA value | 0.01 | engine floor (GroundMoveType); they start and stop faster than in TA |
| Movement classes layout | — | `movedefs_classes.lua` (+ `_tags`, `_post`), as in Splinter Faction | User request. Every class: SF's unit heat (always on; only the legacy pathfinder uses it, QTPFS ignores it), raw movement, flow mapping, SF's `genericDepthmodParams` (submerged tanks/kbots at half speed at any depth; engine default slowed them with depth, 9 % at 100 deep). SF's `slopeMod = 4` default too (much less slowdown on hills than the engine default 4 / maxSlope). Not taken from SF: its footprint override (units keep their own). The post step makes units' own water-depth tags match their class (movement already followed the class) |
| Friendly fire | own and allied units take damage from everything | ordinary weapons do none to your own or allied units (`luarules/gadgets/unit_friendly_fire_removal.lua`, from SF, extended to allies); as in TA, death explosions and self-destructs (mines and crawling bombs included), the D-Gun and nukes (anti-nuke targetable missiles) still hurt everyone; a weapon can opt in with `customparams.friendlyfire = "true"` | User decision |
| `gamedata/modrules.lua` | — | `airmiplevel = 4` | the old `airlosmiplevel` key was not read by Recoil; `losmul`/`airlosmul` removed (not read either) |

Things TA data does that look odd but were kept because they are TA: the Thunder bomber explodes with the Zeus's
lightning, the Geothermal self-destructs like a nuke, flak has no extra damage against aircraft (BOTA had 180 vs
planes), the EMP/Tron one-sidedness above.

## Verification

- `tools/ta-data/run_all.sh` + `compare.py`: no balance differences left except the rows above.
- Runtime def dump before/after (`DUMP=` mode of `tools/headless-test/run.sh`, `defdump.py --diff`): only the intended
  fields changed (plus derived ones: unit cost/power/mass, turn-in-place limit; weapon ids renumbered).
- Headless test: 0 spawn failures, no new warnings. Aim probe (every armed unit fires at ground and air targets):
  every weapon still fires; the Freedom Fighter's missile and the Albatross torpedo hit less in the probe (TA ranges
  300 and 510 vs BOTA's 480 and 630).
- Visuals: `VISUAL=` mode (graphical engine on the GPU), cannons/EMGs/lasers/artillery side by side with main.

## Effects (user decision, 2026-11-07: pretty over TA-accurate)

All of TA:Recoil's old CEGs and effect bitmaps are gone; Splinter Faction's effect library replaces them
(`gamedata/explosions/**` 172 files / 784 effects, `bitmaps/` minus SF's loading pictures, UI art, icons and logo;
TA's `bitmaps/tracks` and `bitmaps/loadpictures` stay). `gamedata/resources.lua` is SF's. Wiring, all in
`gamedata/alldefs_post.lua` and only with effects SF itself uses:

- **Weapon impacts** (`ImpactCEG`, every weapon; user's rules): lasers `burnblacksmall` (a black scorch), except
  very heavy ones (>= 1000 damage: Annihilator, Doomsday, Penetrator, Krogoth, sniper) `genericshellexplosion-large-
  <laser colour>`; light fast-firing plasma (E.M.G.s, Immolator: default damage < 25, reload <= 1 s)
  `burnblacksmall`; other cannons `genericshellexplosion-small/medium/large-orange` by damage and area; anti-air
  (flak and AA missiles: more damage to aircraft than to anything else, or no ground fire) `-small/medium-purple`;
  ground missiles and rockets `-<size>-red`; D-Gun `-small-orange` on each hit; nukes `nukedatbewm(small)`; torpedoes
  `torpedoexplosion`; bombs `genericshellexplosion-bomb(-huge)`; lightning and paralyzers `-electric-small` (Stunner:
  EMP building blast); flamethrowers `burnblacksmall`; anti-nukes `-large-white`. The choice is kept per weapon in
  `customparams.impactceg`.
- **Death explosions** (`DeathExplosions`): TA's explosion weapon (damage, area) is kept and cloned per effect
  (`<weapon>__<effect>`). Size from the unit (cost, footprint; self-destruct one size up). Colour: Arm units blue,
  Core red, aircraft white, ships orange, Krogoth huge purple, commanders and the nuclear mine SF's commander nuke,
  decoys white; buildings: energy blue-EMP (fusion huge white-EMP), metal green, radar/sonar/targeting purple, towers
  red, factories orange, nuke silos white-EMP, Geothermal self-destructs as a nuke (TA). Tables at the top of the
  section, easy to change.
- **Muzzle flashes** (`MuzzleCEG`; spawned by `luarules/gadgets/fx_muzzle.lua` where each shot is created, so no
  unit script needs an EmitSfx): plasma cannons SF's `gdhcannon` flame with a spent casing, energy weapons (lasers,
  lightning, D-Gun) SF's `electricity` flash tinted in the weapon's colour. Both remade in five sizes in
  `gamedata/explosions/tar-muzzleflashes.lua` (`tar-muzzle-cannon-<size>`, `tar-muzzle-energy-<size>-<colour>`),
  sized by damage (E.M.G.s and light lasers tiny ... Big Bertha, Intimidator, Annihilators huge); cannons firing
  faster than once a second one size smaller. Missiles, bombs, torpedoes and flamethrowers have none.
- **Effect sizes** (user request: SF's were far too big for TA's units): shell impacts half of SF's (shock wave and
  ground ring 40 %); unit and building death explosions, bombs and the electric hit resized so the fireball ends
  at about the unit's or building's size (unit radius ~25/40/65/100, building ~35/60/90/140 for small..huge), the
  ground glow ~1.4x that. Checked next to Peewee/Bulldog/Goliath/Krogoth and LLT/energy storage/kbot lab/fusion
  (`VISUALHOLD=1`, `VISUALSPACING=`, `VISUALKILL=1` with `run_win_visual.sh`). SF's values are in the comments.
  Death explosion size: wide mobile hulls (4x4) are at least medium (was large), buildings under 200 metal at most
  medium (solar collectors).
- **Plasma balls**: every Cannon is the engine-default orange (BOTA had tinted some; the E.M.G. was yellow).
- **Damage smoke**: SF's (`blacksmokesmall` + `burnblacksmall` below 90 % health) in `scripts/smokeunit.lua` and the
  converted scripts; commanders, decoys, Crock and Triton now smoke too.
- **Nano**: `luarules/gadgets/fx_nano.lua` puts SF's `nano-animated` on every nano piece while a builder or factory
  sprays (build, repair, reclaim...), next to the engine's own nanospray.

Engine 2026.09.01 issues found and fixed on our side (SF has the same files):
- `CBitmapMuzzleFlame` reads `animParams1`/`animParams2`, not `animParams`: 11 SF muzzle flames drew their whole sprite
  sheet as one flat quad. Fixed in the copied files (both slots set).
- With the default 4096 atlas pages, SF's 41 megapixels of effect textures need several pages, and effects that mix
  pages draw flat garbled rectangles. SF's fix is ported: `LuaIntro/Addons/initial_settings.lua` sets
  `MaxTextureAtlasSizeX/Y = 8192` on a first run while loading, `luaui/widgets/gui_initial_settings.lua` reloads a
  single-player match once if needed (`luaui/configs/initial_settings.lua`: only the atlas keys).
- `bitmaps/default/circles.tga` (seismic ring) was 260x260; non-power-of-two broke the groundFX atlas. Now 256x256.

How to look at effects: `VISUALCEG=name1,name2` / `VISUAL=units [VISUALKILL=1]` with
`tools/headless-test/run_win_visual.sh` (Windows engine from WSL: real GPU colours; the WSL D3D12 bridge blends
particles wrongly).

## Mexes and geovents (2026-11-08)
- Mexes are metal makers on fixed metal spots, as in Splinter Faction (luarules/gadgets/game_metal_spots.lua,
  game_metal_spot_output.lua). Spots are read from the map's metal map as placed (not randomised); each gets
  building mask 4 sized for a moho (5x5 + margin), so only mexes build there and nothing else can.
- Output per spot = 2 x sqrt(players / 2) (1v1 2.0, 4 players 2.83, 8 players 4.0); moho mine 3x (TA ratio).
  Energy upkeep stays TA's (mex 3, moho 15/12, underwater mex 2), only while switched on. Maps without metal spots
  give no mex income.
- Moho mines can be placed over your own basic mex (stackable yardmap); the mex keeps producing until the moho
  finishes, then vanishes (no explosion or wreck; unit_mex_upgrade.lua).
- F4 no longer shows the metal map: it toggles bright rings on the spots (luaui/widgets/game_metal_spot_rings.lua),
  which also appear while a mex is the active build. Spots and geovents are on the minimap.
- Right-click a spot with builders selected queues their best mex (moho if they can); drag to paint a line of spots.
  Right-click a geovent queues a geothermal plant. Geovents are the map's own, marked with a yellow column.
